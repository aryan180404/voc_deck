Overview

The problem

Product teams hear about problems too late

Customer feedback arrives through support emails, chat and call-centre tickets, app stores and LinkedIn. It is scattered and mostly internal, and nobody reads all of it. A serious problem in one district, or a strong feature idea in one post, stays unseen until it is expensive, and product issues get buried under delivery and refund complaints.

Practice data modelled on Zomato, plus live reviews and LinkedIn posts collected each day.

The solution

Collect every complaint, separate product from operations, rank what matters, and track each fix until the complaints stop.

Feature 1 of 5

Live search, LinkedIn first

  • Every day, public LinkedIn posts and articles about Zomato are found and added, each with a link to the original post.
  • LinkedIn counts most. Posts there are specific and addressed to the leadership, so they weigh 1.0, like emails and tickets; app stores and social media weigh 0.4.
  • Imports run through the pipeline too: a post you find yourself is cleaned and labelled like everything else.
LinkedIn

Feature 2 of 5

A copilot that answers from the data

  • Ask in plain words: what to fix first, what changed after a release, what Hindi reviews say about delivery.
  • Every answer cites real messages, and the numbers come from code, never from the model.
  • Follows your view: the Product or Operations switch and the filters shape what it reads.
Ask the copilot
What should we fix first, and why?
Refund and policy disputes lead emails and tickets. Most mention "days refund" (41 of 120) or "rejected reason" (29 of 120), most often in Satellite and Koramangala. Check the default refund method and refund timelines first.

Feature 4 of 5

A control centre to update and track

  • Product managers and the tech team propose solutions, set owners and move them from proposed to resolved, saved in a shared Google Sheet.
  • An escalation matrix says who acts first and who is next when a problem is critical.
  • Resolved is checked: if complaints keep coming, the problem returns as "Resolved but still happening".
Control centre

Feature 5 of 5

A fix for every problem

Each problem gets a problem statement, the exact issue found, a root cause to check, and at most three short fixes ranked by reach, impact and confidence.
